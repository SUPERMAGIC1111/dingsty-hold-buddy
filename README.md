# Dingsty Hold Buddy (demo)

Call a hotline on speaker. Dingsty listens, waits with you, and dances when a person starts talking after the hold music.

- Runs entirely in your browser. The microphone is used on your device only. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
- No account, no cookies, no analytics, no external scripts or fonts.
- Early demo. It guesses "music or talking" from rhythm, so a long recorded announcement can fool it. Tested with synthetic sounds only.
- Use two devices: the call on speaker, this page on a laptop or second phone.

"Run demo (no mic)" shows the whole flow without a call. Not legal advice, not a finished product.
